SkillX

Skills intelligence translated into practical AI learning.

Organisations and employees often can't say which skills matter for a role, where AI could help with its tasks, or how to learn to use it in practice. SkillX combines open occupational and skills data to answer those questions, then connects each answer to learning material and hands-on exercises.

The Problem

Managers and employees hear that AI will change their work, but rarely in terms of their own tasks. Which skills does this role really rely on? Which tasks could AI support, and which not?

Generic AI courses teach tools in the abstract, and traditional learning platforms aren't connected to the work itself. Without that link, training budgets go to courses that don't change how anyone works.

This section will cover open occupational and skills datasets and how they describe roles, tasks and skills; how to judge where AI can realistically support a task; and how adults learn a new practical skill.
