CareerDB

A smarter system for managing the job search.

Job seekers juggle listings, CV versions, applications and email threads across separate platforms. CareerDB brings them into one personal database: it keeps track of opportunities, helps tailor a CV to each role, and records where every application stands, so the search becomes a process you can see and manage.

The Problem

Someone applying for several roles a week keeps job listings in browser tabs, CV versions in folders, and replies in an inbox. Each part lives on a different platform, and none of them knows about the others.

Job boards are built for finding listings, not managing a search. Spreadsheets help until they fall out of date. The cost is real: missed follow-ups, the wrong CV sent, and hours of admin that could go into better applications.
